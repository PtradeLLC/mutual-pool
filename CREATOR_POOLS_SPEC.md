# Creator Pools: Product and Engineering Spec

Status: agreed design, not built yet. Written November 2026.

Creator Pools let a social media creator invite their followers into savings pods with one permanent link. Followers are placed automatically into pods of 20, and a new pod is created whenever the current one fills. The creator hosts every pod and earns 3% of each weekly pot.

Gig and trade worker pods do not change. Everything in this spec applies only to pods that belong to a Creator Pool, unless a section says otherwise.

---

## 1. Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Creator** | A user approved as a creator (section 2). Hosts a Creator Pool. |
| **Follower** | Anyone who joins through a creator's link. |
| **Creator Pool** | A creator's enrollment: one link, one deposit amount, many pods. A creator has at most one pool open for enrollment at a time. |
| **Pod** | A normal MutualPool pod (same object, rules and weekly runs as today) that belongs to a Creator Pool. Pods in a pool are numbered: Pod 1, Pod 2, … |
| **Seated** | A follower who has finished every joining step (section 5) and holds a place in a pod. |
| **Clean completion** | A pod counts as completed for a member who made every deposit through the final week, regardless of what other members did. The server already counts this as `cleanCompletedPods(user)`. |

---

## 2. Becoming a creator

1. The user chooses "I'm a creator" at sign-up or later from their profile.
2. They connect at least one of **Instagram, TikTok or YouTube** through each platform's login. This proves they own the account and gives us the follower count. MutualPool already has developer accounts with Meta, TikTok and Google; the follower-count permissions still need each platform's approval.
   - Instagram: business or creator accounts only (Instagram API with Instagram Login, `followers_count`).
   - TikTok: Login Kit with the `user.info.stats` scope (`follower_count`).
   - YouTube: YouTube Data API `channels.list` (`subscriberCount`; may be hidden by the channel, in which case that account counts as 0).
3. **At least 1,000 followers combined** across connected accounts.
4. **Identity check** through the same Column account opening every member already goes through (`provisionMemberAccount` in [src/server/custody.ts](src/server/custody.ts)). No other identity provider.
5. **Linked bank account** for withdrawals.
6. **Accept the creator terms** (section 10).
7. **W-9** (section 11). A creator can be approved and earn without it, but cannot withdraw earnings until it is on file.

Approval is automatic once steps 2–6 pass. Admins can suspend a creator at any time.

Follower counts are re-read when the creator opens a new pool. A creator who falls below 1,000 keeps running pools but cannot open a new one.

## 3. The Creator Pool

### Settings the creator picks

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| Name and short description | Free text, moderated like pod names today. |
| Weekly deposit | $10, $20, $30, $40, $50, $60 for a creator's first pool. $70, $80, $90 and $100 unlock for later pools, and those pools only accept followers with at least one clean completion. |
| Participate or host only | **Host only** (default): the creator is shown as Member 21 and holds no payout slot. **Participate**: the creator also takes slot 20 of Pod 1 only, and pays deposits there like any member. |

Pod size is always **20**. The creator cannot change it.

### Enrollment

- The creator gets **one permanent link** (`themutualpool.com/c/<handle>`). It always points to that creator's currently open pool and never breaks.
- Enrollment closes when the creator closes it, or automatically **8 weeks** after it opened, whichever comes first.
- When enrollment is closed and there is no open pool, the link shows the creator's profile and a **"Notify me when the next pool opens"** button. Sign-ups go on the creator's waiting list. MutualPool sends the notification; the creator sees the count, not the contact details.
- After closing enrollment, the creator can open their next pool right away. The existing pods keep running until they complete.
- Pods generated by a pool do **not** count toward the 3-created-pods limit that applies to everyone else.

### Pool statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `OPEN` | Accepting followers. |
| `CLOSED` | Enrollment closed; pods are still forming or running. |
| `COMPLETED` | Every pod in the pool has completed. |
| `SUSPENDED` | Creator suspended. Enrollment stops; existing pods keep running (section 9). |

Pods keep the existing statuses (`FORMING`, `LOCKED`, `ACTIVE`, `PAUSED`, `COMPLETED`). There are no separate pod statuses for creator pods. "Open" and "full" are just a forming pod with or without free seats.

## 4. Who can join

- **US only at launch.** Visitors outside the US see "Creator pools aren't available in your country yet."
- **Concurrent limits** (count every creator-pool pod the member is in, forming or running, across all creators; gig pods are not counted and not affected):
  - No clean completion yet: **1** creator-pool pod.
  - At least one clean completion (any pod, gig or creator): up to **3** creator-pool pods at a time. These can be several pods in the same creator's pool.
- Restricted accounts (unpaid missed deposits) cannot join, as today.
- Rules waived for creator pods (see section 13 for the full list): the new-account limit of $5/$10/$20 deposits and the pot-size ladder ($1,000 for members with no completed pod). The pool's deposit options replace them.
- A creator can join another creator's pool as a follower, under the same limits.

## 5. Joining and seat assignment

A follower is **seated** only after all of these, in this order:

1. Sign up or sign in.
2. Identity check through Column (existing).
3. Link a bank account and authorize bank debits (existing debit mandate).
4. Sign the pod agreement (existing).

Nobody holds a seat while unfinished. A follower who abandons sign-up takes no seat.

When a follower finishes step 4, the server seats them in this order:

1. **Held slots first.** An open slot in a running pod of this pool whose previous member was removed before being paid (section 8). If several, the slot with the earliest payout week.
2. **The pool's forming pod.**
3. If there is no forming pod, or it just filled, **create the next pod** and seat them there.

Seating must be atomic: two followers finishing at the same moment can never get the same seat or push a pod past 20. The server is a single process, so this means doing the whole check-and-seat in one synchronous step, guarded the same way the join route guards against duplicate joins today (`JOIN_IN_PROGRESS` in [server.ts](server.ts)).

A follower who takes a held slot in a running pod pays only the remaining weeks and receives that slot's payout. This is intentional.

## 6. Starting pods and payout order

- The creator launches the pool. Every pod, including Pod 1, **starts automatically** as soon as its 20th member is seated. All members have already signed and authorized debits, so nothing else is needed. The creator does not press start on each pod.
- **A pod that stops filling.** If a forming pod gets no new member for **14 days**, or enrollment closes while it is not full:
  1. Its members move into held slots in this pool's running pods, if any.
  2. If members remain and there are at least 2, the pod starts with them, so its cycle and pot are smaller.
  3. A single remaining member is released from the pod and notified.
- **Payout order** is drawn when the pod starts, using the existing `planRotation` in [src/server/rotation.ts](src/server/rotation.ts): ordered by trust level, random within each level. Trust levels for creator pods, highest first:
  1. At least one clean completion.
  2. Gig earnings arriving by direct deposit (`user.earningsDeposit`), which is new as a trust level.
  3. 90+ days on MutualPool.
  4. New with a debit authorization.

  Joining earlier gives no advantage, which removes the reason to race to the link. Before signing, members see "Your payout week is drawn when the pod starts."
- The rule that pots over $1,000 need members with a track record in the first half of the order (`earlySlotsNeedSeasoned`) **does not apply** to creator pods. Nobody is blocked from a slot.
- **Swaps** work as today: a member requests a swap and the other member must agree. In-pod messaging says **"Request a swap to get paid sooner."** The early-slot rule is not checked on creator-pod swaps.

### How the creator appears in each pod

- **Host only:** the 20 followers hold slots 1–20. The creator appears below them as **Member 21** with a **"Last"** badge. Tapping the badge shows: "[Creator] hosts this pod and earns 3% of each weekly pot. They don't take a payout slot."
- **Participating (Pod 1 only):** the creator holds slot 20 with the same badge and explanation; Pod 1 has 19 followers.

## 7. Money

### Fees (unchanged amounts, from `FEES` in [src/lib/legal.ts](src/lib/legal.ts))

| Fee | Amount | Where it goes in creator pods |
|---|---|---|
| Deposit buffer | 5% of every weekly deposit | The pod's contingency buffer. Covers missed deposits. Unused buffer is refunded to members when the pod completes. Same as gig pods. |
| Payout fee | 10% of each weekly pot | **3% to the creator, 7% to MutualPool.** |

- The creator's 3% is paid **every week, with each payout**, into the creator's MutualPool balance. It is calculated on the gross pot, including any part the buffer covered. A paused week pays nothing.
- One commission per pod per week: a retried or replayed payout must never pay it twice. Key the ledger movement on (pod, cycle week).
- If the creator is suspended, banned or deletes their account, the 3% goes to MutualPool for the rest of those pods.
- In gig pods, the 3% still goes to the holder of the last slot, unchanged.

### Payout safety (creator pods)

1. Payouts are paid only from deposits that have settled at Column (existing).
2. **Withdrawals go only to the bank account that was debited** (the debit mandate's account), and the bank account owner's name must match the verified identity.
3. **Keep the debit authorization evidence** for every member: time, IP address, device, and the exact agreement text signed. Used to collect and to report fraud when a member falsely claims a debit was unauthorized.
4. **3-business-day hold on a member's first payout** if they have no clean completion and no gig earnings arriving by direct deposit. The payout reaches their MutualPool balance on time; it can be withdrawn after 3 business days.
5. Missed deposits follow section 8.

**Still open:** members can tell their bank a debit was unauthorized for up to 60 days, which reverses it even after they were paid. The steps above reduce this; keep looking for further protections.

## 8. Missed deposits and removed members

The existing default process applies ([src/server/defaultControls.ts](src/server/defaultControls.ts)):

1. A member misses a deposit. They and everyone in the pod are notified.
2. After **24 hours** unpaid, they are removed from the pod, their account is restricted (no joining, swaps, votes or hardship requests), and we start collecting by debiting their bank. Paying in full lifts the restriction.

**Who can see names:** members who missed deposits are listed **by name inside their own pod** only. Pod members and the creator can see the list, and the creator can message them in the app. Names are never shown across pods or on the creator's pool overview.

What changes for creator pods depends on whether the removed member was already paid:

### Removed before being paid

- Their slot is **held open** (new behavior; today the slot is deleted immediately, see `removeMemberFromRotation` in [server.ts](server.ts)).
- The next follower to finish joining takes it first (section 5). There is no vote.
- If nobody takes it by **7 days before that slot's payout week**, the slot is deleted as the code does today: later members move up a week and the pod ends a week earlier.

### Removed after being paid

- Their slot cannot be refilled. They owe all their remaining deposits, collected from their bank.
- Each week, **the buffer covers the missing deposits**, and every member is notified, as in gig pods today.
- **A vote opens only when the buffer can't cover that week's missing deposits.** It reuses the existing pause vote: **pause** the pod while we collect, or **cover** the shortfall between the members.
- If the vote gets no votes within its 48-hour window, the result is **pause**. Nobody is charged extra without agreeing to it.
- Money collected from the removed member repays whoever fronted it first (existing).

The existing "list the pod publicly" replacement vote is not used for creator pods.

## 9. Creator dashboard

**Pool overview** (totals only, no member names):
- Followers seated, pods forming, running and completed.
- Share of deposits on time this week, and number missed.
- Earnings this week and to date, and withdrawable balance.
- Waiting-list size.
- Enrollment status and days left.

**Pod detail** (one pod at a time): members, payout order and current week, which members missed deposits (by name), and a button to message a member in the app.

**Actions:**
- Close enrollment.
- Open the next pool.
- Post to all pods through a one-way **announcement channel**, so the creator does not have to join every pod chat.

A suspended creator keeps read access but loses enrollment and announcements.

## 10. Chat, safety and creator terms

- Each pod keeps its own chat (existing). Members cannot see each other's email or phone.
- Every creator-pod chat has a pinned message: **"MutualPool and [creator] will never ask you to pay outside the app."**
- **Report** button on messages and members.
- **Creator terms**, accepted at approval:
  1. Disclose the commission **in every post or video that promotes the pool** (e.g. "#ad" or "I earn a commission from this pool"), not only on the profile. Pasteable wording is shown when the creator gets their link.
  2. Don't promise returns or call the pool an investment.
  3. Never ask members for money outside the app.
  4. Don't name members publicly outside the app.
  5. Breaking the terms can lead to suspension (section 3) and loss of future commission.
- The creator's public profile and join page show: "[Creator] earns 3% of each weekly pot for hosting."

## 11. Creator tax forms (in-house for the pilot)

MutualPool pays creators, so it files a **1099-NEC** for each creator paid more than **$2,000 in a calendar year** (the threshold was $600 before 2026).

1. **W-9 in the app:**
   - legal name, business name if any, tax classification, address;
   - SSN or EIN;
   - e-signed certification.

   Withdrawals of creator earnings are blocked until it is on file. Earnings still accrue.
2. **Tax ID storage.** This is the only place the app stores a Social Security number.
   - Encrypt with AES-256-GCM using a dedicated key held as a Fly secret, separate from all other data.
   - Store as its own collection, never inside the user document.
   - Show only the last 4 digits anywhere in the app.
   - Only the year-end export decrypts it.
   - Every access is written to the audit log.
3. **Check the name and tax ID** with the IRS TIN Matching service when the W-9 is submitted.
4. **Each January:**
   - an admin export produces the IRS IRIS upload CSV;
   - someone (founder or accountant) files it;
   - each creator gets their copy by email and in the app.

   Some states require their own filing.
5. Revisit a tax-form service (e.g. Track1099, TaxBandits) once there are dozens of creators, and stop storing tax IDs.

## 12. Launch and capacity

- **Pilot:** 1–2 creators, each pool capped at **1,000 followers**, until it has run cleanly.
- **Queue page** during sign-up surges. One creator post can bring more people at once than the single server handles (about 1,500–2,000 at the same time). Visitors past a set number of joins in progress see "You're #312, we'll hold your place" and continue in order.
- **Before launch:**
  - approval from Column for paying creators and for buffer-covered shortfalls;
  - legal review of the creator terms and disclosures;
  - platform permissions for follower counts.

## 13. Rules that differ from gig pods

| Rule | Gig and trade pods (unchanged) | Creator pods |
|---|---|---|
| Pod size | 10 or 20 | Always 20 |
| Weekly deposit | $5, $10, $20, $50, $100; new accounts limited to $5–$20 | $10–$60 first pool; $70–$100 later pools, completed followers only |
| Pot limit by completed pods | $1,000 / $2,500 / $5,000 / any | Not applied; deposit options above set the limit (max $1,200 first pool, $2,000 later) |
| Early slots in pots over $1,000 need a track record | Yes | No |
| Who receives the 3% | Holder of the last slot | The creator |
| How many pods a member can be in | Not limited by count | 1 until a clean completion, then 3 (creator pods only) |
| Max 3 forming/running pods created per member | Yes | Pool pods don't count toward the creator's limit |
| Who starts the pod | The pod's creator | Automatic when 20 are seated |
| Removed before being paid | Slot deleted; vote on listing the pod publicly | Slot held for the next follower; deleted 7 days before its payout week if unfilled |
| Removed after being paid | Pause vote at 2+ members in default | Buffer covers; vote only when the buffer can't; no votes = pause |
| Missed-deposit names | Pod members see them | Pod members and the creator, inside that pod only |
| First payout | Withdrawable immediately | 3-business-day hold for members with no completion or direct deposit |
| Withdrawal destination | Linked bank | Only the debited bank, name must match |
| Countries | All supported markets | US only |

## 14. Data

Stays on the current storage: in-memory state saved as documents in Postgres (`app_documents`), registered with `registerCollection` in [server.ts](server.ts). No new tables.

**New collections:**
- `creatorProfiles`: approval status, connected accounts and follower counts, terms acceptance, suspension.
- `creatorPools`: settings, status, link handle, open/close dates, pod ids in order, 1,000-follower pilot cap.
- `creatorPoolWaitlist`: follower id, creator id, signed-up date, notified date.
- `creatorTaxForms`: W-9 fields with the tax ID encrypted; last 4 in clear.

**New fields on `Pod`:**
- `creatorPoolId`, `creatorId`, `poolSequence`;
- `heldSlots` (rotation index, payout week, release deadline);
- `lastJoinAt` for the 14-day rule.

**New fields on members:** debit authorization evidence (time, IP, device, agreement version).

**Commission:** the existing `HOST_REWARD` ledger movement, paid to the creator and keyed on (pod, week) so it can't be paid twice.

Moving to real tables is part of the multi-server work in [NEXT_FEATURES.md](NEXT_FEATURES.md), not this feature.

## 16. Acceptance tests

**Creators**
- [ ] A user with under 1,000 combined followers can't be approved; at 1,000 with identity and bank done, approval is automatic.
- [ ] A creator can't open a second pool while one is open; can open one right after closing enrollment.
- [ ] Enrollment closes automatically after 8 weeks.
- [ ] First pool offers only $10–$60. A later pool at $70–$100 rejects followers with no clean completion.
- [ ] Pool pods don't count toward the creator's 3-created-pods limit.
- [ ] Withdrawals of creator earnings are blocked until a W-9 is on file; earnings still accrue.
- [ ] The tax ID is never returned by any API except the admin year-end export, and appears nowhere unencrypted in stored documents.

**Joining**
- [ ] Non-US visitors can't join and see the not-available message.
- [ ] A follower who hasn't finished identity, bank, debit authorization and signing holds no seat.
- [ ] 100 followers finishing at the same moment fill exactly 5 pods of 20, with no duplicates and no pod over 20.
- [ ] A held slot is filled before the forming pod gets the next follower.
- [ ] A follower with no clean completion can't join a second creator-pool pod; after one, can hold 3 and not 4.
- [ ] Gig pod joining and limits behave exactly as before.

**Starting and order**
- [ ] A pod starts automatically when its 20th member is seated.
- [ ] A pod with no new member for 14 days moves its members into held slots, then starts with 2+ or releases a single member.
- [ ] Payout order follows the trust levels; within a level, join time has no effect.
- [ ] A creator pod with a pot over $1,000 and all-new members starts (no track-record block).
- [ ] Host-only creator shows as Member 21 with the "Last" badge; participating creator holds slot 20 of Pod 1 only.

**Money**
- [ ] Each weekly payout pays the creator exactly 3% of the gross pot and MutualPool 7%; a replayed payout pays no second commission.
- [ ] After the creator is suspended or deletes their account, the 3% goes to MutualPool and the pods keep running.
- [ ] A first payout to a member with no completion and no direct deposit can't be withdrawn for 3 business days.
- [ ] Withdrawals only go to the debited bank account, and fail when the owner name doesn't match.

**Missed deposits**
- [ ] An unpaid member removed after 24 hours: slot held, refilled by the next follower; deleted if unfilled 7 days before its payout week.
- [ ] A paid member removed: the buffer covers each week with no vote; the vote opens only in a week the buffer can't cover; no votes in 48 hours means pause.
- [ ] Missed-deposit names appear in that pod for members and the creator, and never on the pool overview.
